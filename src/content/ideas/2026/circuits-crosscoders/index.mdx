Notes on [Sparse Crosscoders for Cross-Layer Features and Model Diffing](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/crosscoders/index.html) (October 2024), a research note in Anthropic's [Transformer Circuits thread](/ideas/2026/transformer-circuits/).

## Core idea

A [sparse autoencoder](/ideas/2026/circuits-monosemanticity/) learns a feature dictionary for one activation site. A **crosscoder** learns a single shared dictionary across several sites at once — multiple layers of one model, or corresponding sites in two related models — so each feature has one activation but separate encoder/decoder directions per site. That shared latent space is what makes two kinds of questions tractable:

- **Cross-layer features:** if features persist across layers in the residual stream, per-layer dictionaries learn duplicated copies of them; a crosscoder can represent each such feature once and show where it is read and written.
- **Model diffing:** trained across two related models (base vs. fine-tuned, checkpoints during training), the dictionary exposes which features are shared, which are model-specific, and which are shared but differently weighted.

## Since the paper

Model diffing is the application that stuck, and it picked up an important correction. Anthropic followed up with [Insights on Crosscoder Model Diffing](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/crosscoder-diffing-update/index.html), and an external group showed that the crosscoder's L1 training loss can *manufacture* apparent differences: concepts present in both models get misattributed as fine-tuned-model-specific through artifacts they call complete shrinkage and latent decoupling. Their fix — latent scaling to flag the artifacts, plus a BatchTopK training variant — recovers latents that really are chat-specific (refusal handling, false information) when diffing Gemma 2 2B base against chat ([Overcoming Sparsity Artifacts in Crosscoders to Interpret Chat-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02922), NeurIPS 2025; there's an accessible [Alignment Forum companion post](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/xmpauEXEerzYcJKNm/what-we-learned-trying-to-diff-base-and-chat-models-and-why)). The cross-layer half of the idea fed directly into the [cross-layer transcoders behind attribution graphs](/ideas/2026/circuits-attribution-graphs/).
